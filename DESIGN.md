# Local Dispatch — Design direction

## Direction

Assigned operate-mode direction translated into a small dispatch archive: the
composer is a drop slot and every saved snippet becomes a searchable local
file.

## Visual system

- Warm paper ground, deep ink, muted blue filing marks, green local-status ink,
  and orange pin marks.
- Karla for the working voice; Courier Prime for archive labels and snippets.
- Ruled paper, numbered sections, stamps, and thin filing lines replace generic
  card chrome.

## Interaction contract

Save, search, pin, copy, delete, clear, and clipboard-read attempts remain
local. Empty, blocked-clipboard, and copied states are explicit.

## Responsive behavior

The drop slot and archive stack on mobile while the archive count remains in
the header as a compact filing index.
